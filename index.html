How to Command Red Dome Outpost

Inside the Dome (Square Plates)

  • [1] Oxygen Tower (45 ore): Keeps dome air pressurized.
  • [2] Water Plant (45 ore): Melts permafrost for crew & farms.
  • [3] Hydroponic Farm (50 ore): Grows Martian tomatoes for food.
  • [4] Habitat Pod (55 ore): Adds +4 crew capacity per tier.

Central Command Hub

  • Tap the central tower to Expand the Dome (MK1 → MK2 → MK3), unlocking up to 24 interior plates and 19 outdoor sand pads.

Across the Sand (Hex Pads)

  • [1] Solar Array (40 ore): Supplies clean colony power.
  • [2] Ore Rig (50 ore): Drills ore (+2× output on glowing orange crystal seams!).
  • [3] Droid Bay (60 ore): Deploys 6-wheeled rovers that weld dome cracks automatically.
  • [4] Defense Tower (65 ore): Fires plasma bolts at attacking Alien Saucers.

Controls

  • Mouse / Touch: Tap any plate, pad, or building; drag to orbit camera; scroll to zoom.
  • Keyboard: 1–4 trigger Build Ring slots, Tab cycles plates/pads, Space pauses, Esc closes ring.
